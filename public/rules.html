Section 1 — Name, Form, and Mission

1.1 Name

The Block by Block AI Safety & Security Alliance (the "Alliance"). Also referred to as "BBA" or "the Alliance" in this document.

1.2 Legal Form

The Alliance operates as an unincorporated governance framework stewarded by Pure Expertz™ LLC (an Illinois limited liability company). Formal entity formation is under evaluation. The Alliance is not a separate legal entity as of v1.6.

1.3 Mission

The Alliance exists to (a) document working governance for AI-enhanced business and civic operations, (b) connect operators, leaders, and institutions under a shared cultural contract (the Three Laws), (c) translate evolving AI legislation and policy into operational practice, and (d) build the institutional bridges that allow AI to multiply strong humans rather than commoditize them.

1.4 Operating Thesis

Smart Systems + Strong Humans. Every Alliance activity reflects the operating thesis that AI multiplies the strong human and commoditizes the weak one. Members of the Alliance are working on being the first kind.

Section 3 — The Three Laws

The cultural contract of the Alliance. Every member, vendor, partner, Independent Consultant, cohort participant, and Founding Triad member acknowledges and operates under the Three Laws.

3.1 Legal

All activity complies with applicable law in the jurisdictions where it occurs.

  • Where law is settled, compliance is straightforward and non-negotiable.
  • Where law is unsettled or evolving (as is common in 2026 AI legislation), Alliance members default to the more conservative interpretation and document their reasoning.
  • Where law conflicts across jurisdictions, members elevate the question through Alliance steward channels before acting.
Application examples: An Independent Consultant operating in a state with new AI-disclosure laws must comply in all client-facing materials. A cohort participant deploying an AI agent in a regulated industry must comply with applicable regulatory frameworks. A Founding Triad member structuring an Investment Group SPV must ensure full compliance with applicable securities laws.

3.2 Ethical

All activity respects the professional ethics applicable to each participant's profession and the broader ethical norms of the markets in which the Alliance operates.

  • Where ethical norms diverge from law, both are addressed independently. Compliance with the law is necessary but not sufficient.
  • Professional ethics (attorneys, real estate professionals, security professionals, healthcare professionals, financial advisors) take precedence over Alliance norms in the practice of those professions.
  • Conflicts of interest, dual representation, and undisclosed material relationships are considered ethical violations regardless of whether they are also legal violations.
Application examples: An Alliance member who is also a licensed attorney must comply with their state bar's ethical rules in any Alliance activity that touches their practice of law. A Trusted Service Provider must disclose any material conflicts of interest to clients before accepting an engagement. Independent Consultants must disclose to prospective clients that they operate under license from Pure Expertz™.

3.3 Moral

All activity considers the impact on the individuals, families, communities, and institutions affected.

  • The Alliance explicitly rejects business activity that delivers economic results at the cost of demonstrable human harm.
  • Members are expected to apply moral reasoning even where law and professional ethics are silent.
  • Where a member is uncertain whether activity passes the moral test, they raise the question through Alliance steward channels before acting.
Application examples: An AI-enhanced workflow that eliminates jobs without offering displaced workers a reasonable transition path fails the moral test. Marketing language that exploits fear, urgency, or emotional vulnerability fails the moral test. Wellness travel offerings that ignore labor conditions of in-country workers or environmental impact fail the moral test.

3.4 The Three Laws Acknowledgment Gate

All formal engagement with the Alliance — Independent Consultant licensing, cohort enrollment, Mastermind participation, Founding Triad activity, Investment Group subscription, Trusted Service Provider directory listing, Hardware Lease Agreement execution, Power Partner candidacy — requires explicit acknowledgment of the Three Laws via the Alliance Acknowledgment Gate at blockbyblockalliance.com/acknowledge.

The Acknowledgment Gate is not a contract by itself. It is a cultural commitment. Subsequent engagement-specific agreements incorporate the acknowledgment by reference.

Section 7 — AI Legislative Compliance Tracking New in v1.6

7.1 Purpose

The Alliance tracks active AI legislation and regulatory activity across the United States and provides status updates to members. The purpose is to keep operators in the ecosystem aware of evolving compliance landscapes that affect their use of AI.

7.2 States Currently Tracked

As of v1.6, the Alliance actively tracks AI legislation in nine (9) states: Illinois (IL) — primary tracking jurisdiction · Florida (FL) · Wisconsin (WI) · Indiana (IN) · Texas (TX) · Colorado (CO) · California (CA) · Washington (WA) · New York (NY). This list will expand as the Alliance grows.

7.3 Data Source and Methodology

The Alliance uses the LegiScan API as the primary source for daily legislative data. Internal analysts review flagged bills, categorize them by stage (introduced → committee → passed → signed → effective), and translate legal-language status into plain-English updates for members.

7.4 Frequency and Format

  • Monthly Legislative Brief — published to Alliance members through community.pureexpertz.ai
  • Material change alerts — when a bill in a tracked state changes stage, members in the affected state receive a same-week alert
  • Quarterly cross-jurisdiction summary — comparing how the tracked states are converging or diverging on AI-policy questions

7.5 CRITICAL: Informational Only — NOT Legal Advice

The Alliance Legislative Tracking is informational and educational only. It is NOT legal advice.

  • (a) The Alliance does NOT advise members on whether specific bills apply to their specific operations
  • (b) The Alliance does NOT advise members on compliance strategies for any specific bill or regulation
  • (c) Members must consult qualified legal counsel licensed in the relevant jurisdiction for any compliance decision
  • (d) Pure Expertz™ LLC is NOT a law firm and does not provide legal services
  • (e) Use of the Alliance Legislative Tracking does NOT create an attorney-client relationship between any party and Pure Expertz™ LLC

7.6 FTC "Deceptive Practice" — National AI-Disclosure Risk

Independent of any state legislation, the Federal Trade Commission Act, Section 5 prohibits "unfair or deceptive acts or practices in or affecting commerce." The FTC has signaled that failure to disclose material AI use in customer-facing communications may be considered a deceptive practice — and this risk applies nationally, regardless of state law.

All Alliance members are required to disclose AI use in client-facing materials — including AI-generated content in marketing, AI-assisted customer service interactions, AI-driven decision processes that affect customers, and any other operational use of AI that a reasonable customer would want to know about.

7.7 Reference Laws (Named for Awareness, NOT Interpreted)

  • AIVIA (Artificial Intelligence Video Interview Act, Illinois)
  • HB 3773 (Illinois) · BIPA (Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act) · Illinois SB 2427
  • HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, federal)
  • FTC Act Section 5 (federal)

Members should engage qualified counsel for any application of these or other laws to their specific operations.

7.8 Alliance Position on the AI Legal R&D Subscription

The AI Legal Pulse subscription product (see Section 10) is the productized form of this tracking work. AI Legal Pulse subscribers receive deeper and more frequent intelligence than the standard Alliance Legislative Brief. AI Legal Pulse remains informational and non-legal-advice; subscribers are reminded to consult their own counsel.

Section 9 — Independent Consultant Program

9.1 Recap

The Pure Expertz™ Independent Consultant program licenses qualified operators to (a) sell Pure Expertz™ products and engagements in their own markets, (b) run rooms under the Pure Expertz™ banner, and (c) earn 50% commission on referred Library, TSP, Academy, and Cohort subscriptions and seats.

9.2 Three Laws Application

  • Independent Consultants must disclose their license status to prospects and clients.
  • Independent Consultants must not represent themselves as employees of Pure Expertz™ or as having authority to bind Pure Expertz™ beyond their license terms.
  • Independent Consultants must maintain client confidentiality consistent with the Alliance Mutual NDA template.

9.3 Recruitment Target

Pure Expertz™ is recruiting 150 Independent Consultants as the Independent Consultant cohort scales through 2026 and 2027.

9.4 IC License Agreement and IC NDA

The terms of Independent Consultant licensing are set forth in the Pure Expertz™ Independent Consultant License Agreement v1 and the Pure Expertz™ IC NDA, both of which are signed by every Independent Consultant before activation.

9.5 Network Equipment Requirement New in v1.6

Independent Consultants are expected to acquire Network Equipment within ninety (90) calendar days of license execution through the Pure Expertz™ Hardware Lease Agreement v1 (or, for ICs outside the United States, through a Node-Only Setup Agreement). The Network Equipment requirement ensures every IC operates with the same operating brain, the same five AI workers, the same Tailscale Node security posture, and the same continuing servicing relationship that defines the Pure Expertz™ ecosystem.

Independent Consultants who fail to acquire Network Equipment within 90 days of license execution are subject to license review under the IC License Agreement.

9.6 The 70/30 Incubator Pattern New in v1.6

Beyond the standard IC tier, Pure Expertz™ offers a 70/30 Incubator Pattern for founders building new ventures inside the ecosystem:

  • The founder owns 70% of the new venture entity
  • Pure Expertz™ LLC owns 30% of the new venture entity
  • Pure Expertz™ provides: Network Equipment via Hardware Lease, 5 AI workers (BiB or CiB configuration), weekly workshop integration during 4-week launch program, Alliance brand affiliation, and the operating playbook
  • The founder provides: time (2+ hours/week during launch program), the idea/opportunity/market, their network, and the initial capital investment

Specific terms are documented in a separate 70/30 Incubator Operating Agreement executed between Pure Expertz™ LLC and the founder, on a per-venture basis.

Section 11 — Trusted Service Provider Directory

11.1 Purpose

The Trusted Service Provider ("TSP") directory is a curated registry of vendors, suppliers, and service providers vetted by Pure Expertz™ for alignment with the Three Laws and service quality. TSP directory membership is structured as a yearly commitment with annual renewal. Material breach of the Three Laws is grounds for removal from the directory.

11.2 Amplification New in v1.6

Trusted Service Providers in good standing become amplified service providers — Pure Expertz™ actively promotes their services to Alliance members, Independent Consultants, and the broader ecosystem through referrals, co-marketing opportunities, and inclusion in the directory. Amplification is contingent on the TSP operating consistently within the Three Laws and following Pure Expertz™ instructions on ecosystem positioning.
